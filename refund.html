Refund Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. General Policy

All purchases on FireMax are final. We do not offer refunds for subscription fees or unused credits.

2. Exceptions

We may offer refunds in the following cases:

- Technical issues that prevented you from using the service for more than 24 hours.

- Duplicate charges due to a billing error.

3. Cancellation

You can cancel your subscription at any time. Your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period. No partial refunds are provided.

4. Contact

To request a refund or report a billing issue, please contact us at: firemax.app@gmail.com

Please include the following information in your email:

- Your account email address

- Date of purchase

- Order ID or transaction number

- Description of the issue

We will review your request and respond within 3 business days. Approved refunds will be processed within 5-10 business days depending on your payment method.
