All purchases made through FireMax, including monthly subscriptions and credit packages, are generally non-refundable. By completing a purchase, you acknowledge and agree to this policy.

We may consider refunds in the following exceptional circumstances:

3. Non-Refundable Cases

Refunds will not be issued in the following cases:

  • You changed your mind after purchase.
  • You did not use your credits during the billing period.

4. Subscription Cancellation

You may cancel your subscription at any time through Google Play Store settings. Upon cancellation:

  • Your subscription will remain active until the end of the current billing period.
  • No partial refunds will be provided for the remaining days of the billing period.
  • Unused credits at the time of cancellation will expire at the end of the billing period.

5. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact us at firemax.app@gmail.com with the following information:

  • Your account email address.
  • Date of purchase.
  • Transaction ID or Order ID from Google Play.
  • A detailed description of the issue.
  • Any relevant screenshots or evidence.

6. Refund Processing

Once we receive your refund request:

  • We will review your request within 3 business days.
  • You will receive a response confirming approval or denial of your request.
  • Approved refunds will be processed within 5-10 business days.
